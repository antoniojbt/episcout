# Independent-Truth Review

## Scope and conclusion

- Review date: 2026-07-27
- Reviewer: Codex, with independent parallel sub-reviews of statistics/dates, EDA/reporting/plots, and fixture provenance/data transformations
- Paths and outputs reviewed: `R/eda_*.R`, `R/run_eda.R`, `R/summary_cores.R`, material `R/epi_stats_*.R`, material cleaning/join/recode/import/subsampling/survival/plot functions, `tests/testthat/`, `tests/testthat/fixtures/`, `tests/testthat/_snaps/`, `data-raw/test-fixtures/`, `inst/report-template/`, `inst/project-template/`, relevant `future/specs/`, package documentation, and git history
- Overall conclusion: The repository has useful independent anchors for ordinary missingness, EDA v1 numeric and categorical summaries, row/column NA percentages, and small character/factor/recode cases ([TR-006](truth_review_catalog.csv)-[TR-009](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-013](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-028](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-033](truth_review_catalog.csv)), but it does not establish independent truth for every material analytical output. High-priority gaps remain in analysis-window denominators, 2x2 and NxN contingency calculations, schema semantics, EDA plot content, rendered reports, repeated-measure reshaping, full-outer joins, and transpose labels ([TR-003](truth_review_catalog.csv)-[TR-005](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-016](truth_review_catalog.csv)-[TR-017](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-021](truth_review_catalog.csv)-[TR-024](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-030](truth_review_catalog.csv)-[TR-032](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Several tests preserve internal agreement or current behavior without proving correctness.
- Important limitations: No confidential source system or unpublished benchmark was available. Both committed fixture datasets originate from public R packages, but `tests/testthat/fixtures/blood_storage/SOURCE.md` does not state a dataset-specific licence, so no record-level clinical values are reproduced here ([TR-001](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Visual snapshots were not treated as correctness evidence and were disabled by `tests/testthat/test-plotting.R:11` in the executed suite ([TR-020](truth_review_catalog.csv)).

## Methods

The review followed `future/reviews/truth_review.zip` and classified each catalogue row as `external_independent`, `hand_derived`, `cross_implementation`, `internal_consistency`, `smoke_only`, or `none`. A fixture or expected CSV was not accepted as independent merely because it was committed or labelled independent; its creation path was traced through `data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R`, tests, source notes, git log/blame, and the originating commits.

Inspection used `rg`, `nl`, `git log`, `git show`, `git blame`, SHA-256 reads, targeted read-only R probes, CSV validation, and `scripts/rscript_env_caller.R -e "options(repos = c(CRAN = 'https://cloud.r-project.org')); devtools::test(reporter = 'summary')"`. The suite completed with no test failures, two skips, and one parallel-worker warning; the visual snapshot file was skipped unconditionally at `tests/testthat/test-plotting.R:11`. Testthat attempted to prune those skipped snapshots, so the tracked SVGs were immediately restored and repository state was rechecked; no code, test, fixture, or snapshot change remains.

The claimed raw-fixture origins were independently verified during the review by downloading the exact stated [palmerpenguins 0.1.1](https://cran.r-project.org/src/contrib/palmerpenguins_0.1.1.tar.gz) and [medicaldata 0.2.0](https://cran.r-project.org/src/contrib/medicaldata_0.2.0.tar.gz) CRAN sources into `/private/tmp`. The official `palmerpenguins/inst/extdata/penguins_raw.csv` and `medicaldata/data/blood_storage.rda` matched the committed fixture dimensions, names, and serialized values. This supports raw input provenance ([TR-001](truth_review_catalog.csv)); it does not convert repository-computed expected summaries into external authoritative results.

No confidential data prevented structural inspection. The review did not infer clinical or policy impact, and it did not change implementation, tests, fixtures, or snapshots.

## Findings

1. **Analysis-window and contingency denominators are not independently established.** `epi_stats_prop_outcome()` counts all events at `R/epi_stats_prop_outcome.R:31` but restricts only the denominator at `R/epi_stats_prop_outcome.R:34`; its sole fixture at `tests/testthat/test-missing-functions.R:38` places every event in the tested window, so it cannot discriminate cross-window leakage ([TR-021](truth_review_catalog.csv)). The 2x2 tests at `tests/testthat/test-combined_contingency_functions.R:28` and `:89` check structure and p-value presence rather than exact cells, missing-data denominators, estimates, methods, or known p-values, including the Fisher-to-chi-square override at `R/epi_stats_contingency_2x2.R:120` ([TR-022](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-023](truth_review_catalog.csv)). NxN tests reconcile marginal totals and sum-to-100 invariants against the same input but do not independently specify exact cells, exclusions, zero denominators, or the forced Yes/No levels in `R/epi_stats_contigency_nxn.R:49` ([TR-024](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a small hand table with in-window and out-of-window events, explicit missing rows, exact 2x2/NxN cells and denominators, and independently sourced inferential results.

2. **Three transformation tests either preserve contradicted behavior or depend on the implementation's own output.** `epi_clean_transpose()` removes `id_col_num` from the data at `R/epi_clean_transpose.R:54` but labels rows with `cols[-1]` at `:58`; the `id_col_num=6` test explicitly expects shifted labels at `tests/testthat/test-cleaning_functions.R:356-373` ([TR-030](truth_review_catalog.csv)). The public full-outer claim at `R/epi_clean_merge_nested_dfs.R:3-7` conflicts with the `all.x=TRUE` default/calls at `:116-190`, while tests use overlapping IDs or inspect suffixes, dimensions, and order rather than right-only preservation ([TR-031](truth_review_catalog.csv)). The long-to-wide tests compare seeded `str()` fragments and feed `epi_clean_spread_repeated()` output into its merge test, without an independently authored expected table for unbalanced visits, duplicate ID/visit pairs, zero/nonconsecutive visit codes, missing visits, or unmatched IDs (`R/epi_clean_spread_repeated.R:38-54`; `tests/testthat/test-cleaning_functions.R:268-302`) ([TR-032](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a hand-authored long input and wide output with arbitrary ID-column position, unmatched IDs, and deliberately difficult visit codes.

3. **EDA plot objects and rendered reports are structurally exercised but not truth-reconciled.** Plot dispatch has a useful separate layer-class reference in `data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:97-105` and `tests/testthat/test-penguins-raw-fixtures.R:159-176`, but that rule closely parallels `R/eda_plots.R:42-58` and establishes only histogram-versus-bar dispatch ([TR-015](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Plot data are taken directly from raw columns at `R/eda_plots.R:38-58`, so sentinel codes excluded by `R/eda_missing.R:18-25` and `R/eda_summaries.R:44-74` can remain plotted; no test checks plot-layer data, bin counts, category counts, labels, temporal axes, or appearance ([TR-016](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Report tests at `tests/testthat/test-eda_report.R:40-129` search for generic headings and artifact existence while `inst/report-template/eda.qmd:18-56` prints runtime objects directly, so no rendered cell, denominator, date, skip reason, or plot is reconciled independently ([TR-017](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Machine-readable output checks are likewise existence/header tests rather than value/type round trips ([TR-019](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is independently expected layer data/counts plus a small rendered-report reconciliation for selected cells and serialized values.

4. **Schema/type expectations are partly circular and status does not mean type conformance.** The fixture classifier at `data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:21-41` reproduces the ordered decision tree in `R/eda_schema.R:73-98`; the anti-circularity guard at `tests/testthat/test-fixture-generation-guardrails.R:15-33` detects direct package calls but not copied logic ([TR-002](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-004](truth_review_catalog.csv)). The semantic dictionaries are authored in the same generator and then hard-coded by tests without a field-level upstream reconciliation (`data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:321-410`; `tests/testthat/test-penguins-raw-fixtures.R:27-45,87-96`) ([TR-003](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Schema status is based only on presence at `R/eda_schema.R:38`; `tests/testthat/test-eda_schema-fixtures.R:60-78` accepts expected integer, observed numeric, and status present, so the tested output cannot support a claim of type compatibility ([TR-005](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a source-to-field specification record and hand-authored mismatch cases that state whether each declared/observed pairing conforms.

5. **Several extended statistical values have gating or perfect-case tests but no numerical reference truth.** Common finite quartiles/fences/outliers have small hand evidence, but `na.rm` is accepted yet unused at `R/epi_stats_numeric.R:51-65`, and non-finite denominators and broader outlier cases remain partial ([TR-011](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Skewness, kurtosis, and Shapiro-Wilk checks assert numeric/NA states rather than known values (`R/summary_cores.R:61-75`; `tests/testthat/test-epi_stats_numeric.R:48-84`) ([TR-012](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Correlations are anchored only by a perfect linear `r=1` case, while p-values, pairwise missingness, triangle relabelling, and heatmap cell placement remain same-family transformations (`R/epi_stats_corr.R:40-78`; `tests/testthat/test-stats_corr_and_heatmap.R:50-78`) ([TR-025](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-026](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a documented independent implementation or fixed hand/reference dataset with non-perfect coefficients, p-values, and exact shape/normality values.

6. **Date/time and automated type-inference coverage does not reach boundary behavior.** Small Date/UTC fixtures independently establish endpoints, simple ranges, parsing, and timezone representation, but not temporal quartiles, mode/IQR, ambiguous strings, or local/DST boundaries (`R/summary_cores.R:167-280`; `tests/testthat/test-eda-summaries-v2.R:77-87,129-145`) ([TR-014](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Monthly frequencies have two literal counts, but the difference expectation mirrors `diff(sort(as.numeric()))`, and missing dates, year boundaries, timezone effects, and plotted values are absent (`R/epi_stats_dates.R:111-121`; `tests/testthat/test-stats-dates.R:125-133`) ([TR-027](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Import/type inference checks cover only simple integers and custom NA strings, leaving dates, datetimes, leading-zero identifiers, mixed columns, locale, and sampling-dependent inference without a defined expected contract (`R/epi_read.R:33-51`; `tests/testthat/test-more_coverage.R:55-62`) ([TR-034](truth_review_catalog.csv)); low-cardinality factor inference and CURP century derivation are similarly partial ([TR-035](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-037](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a boundary-focused fixture with explicit types and expected timestamps, including missing and ambiguous cases.

7. **Visual snapshots preserve historical output but do not establish visual correctness, and they currently do not execute.** The SVGs under `tests/testthat/_snaps/plotting/` were generated from earlier package output, their introducing history records no independent visual reference, and `tests/testthat/test-plotting.R:11` skips every vdiffr assertion ([TR-020](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Object-layer tests elsewhere remain useful structural checks, but a consistently wrong plot can satisfy them. Minimum evidence needed after approval is an explicit visual/data contract and an enabled review path; merely regenerating snapshots from current production output would remain internal consistency.

8. **Some exclusions, grouping, and derived-field contracts are only partially discriminated.** `epi_stats_summary()` and `epi_stats_tidy()` have transparent sentinel and 5/10 percentage cases, but caller-supplied `perc_n` is not reconciled to the eligible population after exclusions (`R/epi_stats_summary.R:25-105`; `R/epi_stats_tidy.R:25-71`) ([TR-029](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Duplicate extraction uses `table()`, which excludes missing identifiers, without a test defining intended NA-ID behavior (`R/epi_clean_get_dups.R:32-41`; `tests/testthat/test-cleaning_functions.R:40-60`) ([TR-036](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Stratified sampling checks expected 80/20 counts and uniqueness but not missing outcomes, small strata, or rounding rules (`R/epi_sub_sample.R:16-48`; `tests/testthat/test-epi_sub_sample.R:3-19`) ([TR-038](truth_review_catalog.csv)). These are narrower than the high-priority contradictions above, but they remain material denominator/exclusion decisions.

9. **Integration and survival-plot checks do not upgrade component calculations to independent end-to-end truth.** `epi_eda_run()` is compared directly with the same package component functions at `tests/testthat/test-run_eda-fixtures.R:27-42`, which verifies wiring but cannot detect a shared component error (`R/run_eda.R:16-50`) ([TR-018](truth_review_catalog.csv)). The survival conversion/plot test constructs `survival::survfit()` output but asserts only row count, column presence, ggplot class, and file existence at `tests/testthat/test-epi_plot_km.R:1-18`; it checks no survival estimate, risk set, censor count, stratum parse, confidence bound, or step coordinate (`R/epi_plot_km.R:17-116`) ([TR-039](truth_review_catalog.csv)). Minimum evidence needed after approval is a small end-to-end EDA object and a fixed survival example with independently specified numerical and plotting coordinates.

## Existing strengths

- Raw fixture provenance is real rather than merely asserted: `data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:108-163,264-319` exports named public package objects, and the independent temporary CRAN comparison matched both committed datasets ([TR-001](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
- Ordinary missingness is compared against a separate base-R implementation over two pinned datasets, while sentinel and zero-row behavior use transparent hand fixtures (`data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:43-51`; `tests/testthat/test-eda_missing-fixtures.R:11-103`) ([TR-006](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-007](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
- EDA v1 numeric and categorical summaries have both hand-derived edge cases and separate base-R expected CSVs; the categorical fixture explicitly distinguishes total-row and observed-row denominators (`data-raw/test-fixtures/make_external_fixtures.R:53-95,438-448`; `tests/testthat/test-eda_summaries-fixtures.R:22-88`) ([TR-008](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-009](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
- Character, factor, and text summaries use small readable fixtures with exact counts, lengths, whitespace/empty distinctions, ordering, top counts, and unused levels (`tests/testthat/test-epi_stats_chars.R:13-73`; `tests/testthat/test-epi_stats_factors.R:9-70`; `tests/testthat/test-summary-cores.R:47-53`) ([TR-013](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
- Row/column ordinary-NA percentages and lookup recoding have straightforward expected results rather than only shape assertions (`tests/testthat/test-stats.R:109-131`; `tests/testthat/test-clean-factor_labels.R:37-89`) ([TR-028](truth_review_catalog.csv), [TR-033](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
- Synthetic EDA tests correctly constrain names, row counts, declared levels, integer bounds, zero-row behavior, and deterministic seeds, and project documentation explicitly limits synthetic data to workflow preparation rather than inference (`tests/testthat/test-eda_synthetic-fixtures.R:27-128`; `vignettes/specification-first-eda.Rmd:36`) ([TR-040](truth_review_catalog.csv)).
