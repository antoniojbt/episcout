# Render and Release Checklist

Apply this checklist to rendered vignettes, HTML reports, package tarballs and release artifacts.

## Blocking checks

- [ ] Compare the exact artefact with the original requested end goal and required deliverables. A polished, complete, internally consistent render fails if it changes, narrows, defers or substitutes any unauthorised part.
- [ ] Generate the deliverable from the intended current source and inspect the exact artifact intended for handoff or release.
- [ ] Preserve a requested prior version, confirm the intended baseline and inspect the version-to-version diff for requested changes and unrelated edits.
- [ ] Check every relevant page or view for missing content, overflow, clipping, overlap, broken fonts, corrupt symbols, misplaced objects, blank pages and failed navigation.
- [ ] Confirm that tables, figures, captions, references, headings and cross-references are complete and correctly associated.
- [ ] Compare important rendered facts and numbers with their verified source.
- [ ] Reject stale or nominal-only revisions: a successful render or new filename does not establish that requested results changed.
- [ ] Check filenames, metadata, comments, hidden content, accessibility, confidentiality, disclosure risk, source permissions and licensing as applicable.
- [ ] Confirm output paths and overwrite behaviour, and obtain approval before publication or submission.

## Quality checks

- [ ] Review legibility, hierarchy, spacing, alignment, consistency, colour contrast, image quality and behaviour at the intended viewing size.
- [ ] Check document pagination, report navigation and responsive or interactive states where applicable.
- [ ] Open the deliverable with an appropriate viewer or renderer and note untested platform or format limitations.

## Evidence to report

Report the exact artifact inspected, rendering or viewing method, pages or states checked, corrected defects, remaining limitations and release decision. State when the review was a self-check rather than independent approval.
